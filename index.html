Business lookup

Google will not issue a key without a billing account. The key travels in this app, so restrict it to and set a daily quota cap in the Cloud Console — a cap is what makes a charge impossible rather than merely unlikely.

Return reminders

Events go to the calendar of whichever Google account is signed in here. Turning this on asks Google for permission to add calendar events, which the app does not otherwise need — it only ever creates the ones you tap for, and never reads your calendar. You can leave this on No and use the button on a receipt instead; it asks for itself.
